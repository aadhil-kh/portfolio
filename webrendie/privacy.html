Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains how WebRendie, developed by Mohammed Aadhil, handles information when you use the app.

WebRendie edits and previews web projects locally. Project files are not uploaded to the developer by the editor. Optional CDN features, previewed code, and purchases or license activation can connect to external services.

Projects and local storage

The app keeps project source code, assets, mock API data, project metadata, global assets and collections, settings, and recent-file references on your device. It accesses files or folders you select. Preview console output is processed locally and may contain information logged by your code.

If you open or save files through iCloud Drive or another file provider, that provider may store or synchronize them under its own settings and privacy policy. System backups may also include local app data. These services are controlled by your operating system and chosen providers.

CDN libraries and preview network requests

Opening Add CDN Library can contact the cdnjs API to load its library list. Library searches send your search terms to that API. Loading a selected library can contact its CDN. These services receive your IP address and request information; Cloudflare describes its handling in its privacy policy.

Previewed HTML, CSS, and JavaScript can load remote resources and send requests to destinations specified by your code, examples, or dependencies. JSX module imports may use esm.sh. Those services can receive request information and any data your code sends. Their policies apply to those connections. Review project code and external URLs before previewing sensitive content.

Local preview servers

On macOS, local preview and folder-serving features bind to the loopback address, so other programs on the same Mac can access the served content. They do not publish your project to the internet. Stop the server when you no longer need it.

App Store purchases

Apple processes App Store purchases and restores. The app uses StoreKit to retrieve products and prices and verify your Pro entitlement. The developer does not receive your payment card details through the app. Apple handles purchase information under its App Store privacy notice.

App Store builds use Apple’s purchase system and do not include the direct-distribution license-key service described below.

Direct-distribution Mac licenses

The Mac build distributed outside the App Store uses Dodo Payments for checkout and Pro license activation. Checkout may collect contact, billing, payment, and transaction information. The developer may receive customer and order information needed to provide the purchase and support. Payment handling is described in Dodo Payments’ privacy policy; payment card details are not collected by the editor.

Activation sends your license key and a label containing the app version, macOS platform, and your computer name to Dodo Payments. Validation and deactivation send the license key and activation-instance identifier. Network requests also disclose your IP address to the service. These requests verify and manage the devices using your license.

The app locally stores the license key, activation identifier, product information, customer email if returned by the service, activation and validation dates, and revocation state. An existing activation may be revalidated at launch when its last validation is at least seven days old. Project source code and assets are not included in license requests.

Use Settings → Pro → Deactivate This Mac to release the activation and remove its local record after the service confirms. This does not delete purchase records held by Dodo Payments. Contact support or Dodo Payments about those records; they may retain information needed for transaction and legal obligations.

Retention, deletion, and sharing

Projects, assets, mock data, and settings remain until you delete them or remove the app’s stored data. Deleting a project does not remove separately stored global assets or files saved outside the project. Remove external files, exports, and backups separately. On macOS, removing the application alone can leave its support files and preferences.

Exporting, copying, printing, or sharing content makes it available to the destination you choose. Exported files and clipboard contents can remain outside the app after you delete the original item. Remove those copies separately, including any backups or synchronized copies.

Analytics and tracking

Apple may provide app usage statistics and diagnostics according to your device sharing settings. These are handled under Apple’s App Analytics privacy notice. The app does not include a separate analytics, advertising, or tracking service.

Support and privacy requests

If you contact Mohammed Aadhil at me@aadhil.dev, your email address, message, and any attachments are used to respond to your request and resolve the issue. Email providers process that correspondence to deliver and store it. Support correspondence is kept as needed to handle your request and any related legal obligations; you can request deletion by email.

Please avoid including passwords, license keys, confidential documents, or other sensitive content unless you deliberately choose to share it for support. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information held about you. Contact the same address to exercise those rights. The developer cannot access or remotely delete data stored only on your device.

Children’s privacy

This app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. If you believe a child has supplied personal information in a support request, contact me@aadhil.dev to request its removal.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be available at this URL with a revised date. This policy covers the Apple-platform app and its related support; websites and services you choose to use have their own privacy practices.
