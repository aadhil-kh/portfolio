## New

- **Ad blocking.** Pages in every split have ads and trackers blocked by default, using EasyList and EasyPrivacy.
- **Filter lists.** A Filter lists dialog in Settings lets you choose which lists to use. Each list refreshes on the schedule its publisher sets.
- **Optional lists.** Fanboy's Annoyances and Fanboy's Social Blocking are available but off by default. They block share buttons, comment widgets and embedded social content, which some people want.
- **Blocker status in Settings.** The ad-blocking card shows how many rules are loaded, how many requests were blocked, and any problem with the last update.
